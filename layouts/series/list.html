This is an experiment. Most data content is made either for analysts deep in the weeds or for executives who want the five-slide version. The gap between Excel users, modern-data-stack technocrats, and management is where most data projects actually fail.

This series tries to bridge that gap: short, honest videos about real data problems that anyone in the data chain can watch and actually learn from, regardless of where they sit in the org chart.
