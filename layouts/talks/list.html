About the speaker

Amit Kohli is the Head of Data at Access Social Care, where he builds knowledge graphs and data systems for the social care sector. His career spans UN water resources, international development (ACDI/VOCA, CRS), and data science consulting.

He has spoken at EARL, LegalGeek, Humentum, MERL Tech, Charity Digital, and numerous other events. Topics range from RAG and knowledge graphs to data governance and making international development data not useless.
