This week: building the knowledge graph for this entire series.

I've been mapping every concept, relationship, and video in this series as a live knowledge graph. 43 nodes, 88 relationships, all queryable.

**Why graph it?**

- Shows how tactical Excel pain connects to AI readiness
- Makes the series structure visible (Track A → Track B → Track C)
- Demonstrates knowledge graphs in action (not just theory)

Built with Neo4j, deployed to Netlify, visualised with vis-network.js. 48 more weeks to go - the graph grows every video.
