abstract: 01/02/2026 could be the 1st of February or the 2nd of January, depending which country wrote it. Only the 1st-12th of any month is actually ambiguous - everything after that self-checks, which is exactly why the risky dates slip past a spot check. The fix is year-first dates, the ISO 8601 standard - unambiguous everywhere, and it sorts correctly as plain text too.
