abstract: >
  Somewhere in your pipeline is a CASE WHEN that decided, at some point, whether a client who changed local authority mid-year counts once or twice. Nobody sat down and agreed that - it's just what fixed a bug on a Friday afternoon, and it's been quietly deciding policy ever since. A data dictionary won't catch this: it documents what a field is, not why a decision resolves the way it does. The fix is a decision spec - a document a human can read and argue with - that the code translates, not invents.
