abstract: >
  Same person rings the helpline Monday about a housing issue, rings again Thursday about something else, then again Friday because no one called back. Three rows in the table, but only one person calling about two issues. Nothing looks broken from a data engineering point of view, which is exactly the trap - especially once AI is the one counting the rows for you. This video is about grain: knowing what one row of a table actually represents before you trust a count built on top of it.
