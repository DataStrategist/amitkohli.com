Last week: who should decide what your data means, and why that's a governance question. This week: back to ground level. A problem I've seen in every team I've ever worked with.

I have a diagnosis. Same one, every time.

The broken pipeline. The misaligned strategy. The meeting where three people walked out with three different understandings of what was decided.

Root cause? Nine times out of ten?

Nobody wrote it down.

Not because they were lazy. Not even because they forgot. Sometimes because they didn't know what was going to happen - and didn't want anyone to find out.

That one's worth sitting with.

What's the thing you wish past-you had written down?
