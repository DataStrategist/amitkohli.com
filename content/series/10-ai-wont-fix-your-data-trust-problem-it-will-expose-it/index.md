Jose was manually fixing the data for the Powerpoints that managers see. The Thursday dataset stopped arriving when its owner left three months ago. Nobody noticed - because humans are good at patching around bad data silently. AI isn't. This is what makes AI projects useful: not that they fix your governance problems, but that they make those problems impossible to ignore.
