Last week: built a naïve knowledge graph connecting videos to concepts. This week: why flat classification doesn't scale - and what better structure actually means.

---

**Problem 1 - Different words, same meaning.** Measurement bias. KPIs as targets. Definition drift. Three labels, one structural distortion. If we don't recognise synonyms, we lose signal.

**Problem 2 - Same word, different meaning.** "Responsible party" means governance ownership in one context, programme accountability in another. If we don't make that explicit, ambiguity just gets absorbed into the system.

**Problem 3 - Different kinds of things treated as equals.** Spreadsheet. Excel. Data governance. Trust collapse. Tool. Platform. Capability. Outcome. Flat classification treats them as peers. They're not.

---

That's why flat classification fails. It doesn't force you to ask: what *kind* of thing is this? Tool? Capability? Outcome? Synonym? Subtype?

Until you answer that, you don't have structure. You have a pile of words.

And if your thinking is a pile of words, your AI will learn from that pile perfectly.
