Your good idea works beautifully. For you. The moment someone else tries to use it, things start breaking in ways you can't see - because every solution you build carries assumptions you don't know you're making. That's not a user problem. That's the beginning of product design. PoC answers "can this be done?" MVP answers "can someone else use this?" The gap between them is where most data tools quietly fail.
