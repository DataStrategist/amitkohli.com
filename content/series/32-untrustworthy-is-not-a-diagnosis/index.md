abstract: >
  A dashboard comes back saying the opposite of what the director expected. She asks to check the data - it's checked twice, and it's correct. Six weeks later the number's still sitting there, tagged "data quality issue, revisit next quarter," because nobody's actually worked out whether the data was wrong, the process was wrong, or something upstream broke. What actually happened is the number lost its trust, not its accuracy - and once a metric gets labelled untrustworthy, it stays that way long after the question that created the label gets forgotten. The real cost isn't the bug, it's the trust an org loses every time a disputed number gets shelved instead of resolved.
