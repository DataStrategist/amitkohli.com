abstract: >
  "Quick question - can you just add this to the report?" You say yes, do it by hand, and nobody writes it down. Ask again next week, and it quietly becomes a standing process running on goodwill and one person's memory, invisible until that person is on leave. The fix isn't refusing quick questions - it's naming the ones that repeat. Second time you're asked for the same thing, it's not a favour anymore, it's a requirement with an owner.
