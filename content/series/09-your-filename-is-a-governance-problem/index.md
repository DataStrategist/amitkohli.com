Show me your Downloads folder. I'll tell you exactly how mature your data governance is.

You know the file. `final_v2_REAL_final.xlsx`. Maybe it's `report_FINAL_actualfinal_USE_THIS_ONE.xlsx`. Or the classic: `analysis_v3_AMIT_edits_v2.xlsx`.

Every organisation has this file. It's not embarrassing. It's diagnostic.

Because the filename is a symptom. It means nobody agreed on what a version IS, who OWNS it, or what DONE looks like. So all that information got stuffed into the filename instead. The filename became documentation for a system that has no documentation.

The fix isn't Git. Two things:

1. ISO date: `report_2026-03-04.xlsx` - sorts correctly, no conversation needed
2. Status tag: `_draft` / `_submitted` / `_approved`

If you want the full audit trail - why it changed, who touched it - that lives in a changelog tab inside the workbook.

Filename is for navigation. Tab is for archaeology.

What does your Downloads folder say about your governance?
