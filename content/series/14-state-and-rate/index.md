What do love turning to hate and your bank account running dry have in common? Two numbers. Most dashboards only show you one of them. The state is the photo - how much of something is there right now. The rate is the early warning system - which direction is it heading, and how fast. Once you see this pattern, you can't unsee it in any system you work with.
