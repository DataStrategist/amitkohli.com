## Hi, I'm Amit

I build data systems that make complex information actually usable - knowledge graphs, AI pipelines, data portals, and the awkward infrastructure in between.

My career has taken me from **UN water resources** (mapping water quality and governance data across 30+ countries for AQUASTAT), through international development organisations like **ACDI/VOCA** and **CRS**, into the world of **social care** in England, where I now lead the data function at [Access Social Care](https://www.accesscharity.org.uk).

Along the way I've trained NGO teams on data collection, built M&E systems in fragile contexts, consulted for charities and housing providers, and (increasingly) become the person in the room asking hard questions about what AI is actually doing to the people it's supposed to help.

## What I actually do

- **Knowledge graphs** - I've built a 200+ concept ontology connecting technologies, domains, and projects, and I use Neo4j and graph traversal to navigate and extend it continuously.
- **AI infrastructure** - RAG architectures, prompt engineering, LLM evaluation, and integrating AI into real workflows where "it works in the demo" isn't enough.
- **Data portals** - At Access Social Care I led the design and delivery of a public-facing data portal making social care datasets accessible to advice workers, researchers, and people with lived experience.
- **Data strategy** - I've written and led data strategies for charities, local authorities, and social care providers, connecting high-level vision to what engineers actually build on a Monday.

## How I describe myself

A **data generalist** and **systems thinker**. I move between wrangling messy CSVs and designing ontologies, between writing Python and presenting to boards. I think the most useful thing I do is translate between the people who hold data and the people who need it.

I love [R](https://www.r-project.org/), I'm paying close attention to knowledge graphs and LLMs, and when I'm not working I'm either building things with a CNC mill, playing video games, or trying to find someone to throw a frisbee at.

## Speaking

I speak at conferences and events on data strategy, knowledge graphs, RAG, AI in the public sector, and the messy reality of making data work. Past events include EARL, LegalGeek, Humentum DC, MERL Tech, Charity Digital, LondonR, and AI Engineers UK.
