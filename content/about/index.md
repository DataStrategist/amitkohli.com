## Hi, I'm Amit

I'm a data generalist and systems thinker - someone who moves between wrangling messy CSVs and designing ontologies, between writing Python and presenting to boards. The most useful thing I do is translate between the people who hold data and the people who need it.

My career has spanned the UN, international development NGOs, UK government, and the social care sector. I've been a data scientist, a trainer, a consultant, and (increasingly) the person in the room asking hard questions about what AI is actually doing to the people it's supposed to help.

Right now I'm **Head of Data Insights at [Access Social Care](https://www.accesscharity.org.uk)**, where I lead data science, engineering, governance, and strategy. I built our data portal - making fragmented social care information accessible to advice workers, researchers, and people with lived experience - and I represent the organisation externally on data and AI.

Before that: **Data Director at ACDI/VOCA** in Washington DC (2015-2022), a large international development NGO where I led data functions across programmes in 30+ countries. Before that: **Data Scientist at UN agencies** including ITU and OHCHR, and stints at the Ministry of Justice. I've also done contracted work for the University of Warwick, Recast, and various consultancies.

I love [R](https://www.r-project.org/), I build knowledge graphs for fun, and when I'm not working I'm either milling things on a CNC machine, playing video games, or trying to find someone to throw a frisbee at.

## What I actually do

- **Knowledge graphs** - Built a 200+ concept ontology connecting technologies, domains, and projects. I use Neo4j and graph traversal to navigate and extend it continuously.
- **AI infrastructure** - RAG architectures, prompt engineering, LLM evaluation, and integrating AI into real workflows where "it works in the demo" isn't enough.
- **Data portals and strategy** - Led the design and delivery of a public-facing social care data portal. I've also written data strategies for charities, local authorities, and care providers.
- **Translation** - Turning technical work into language boards, funders, and frontline workers can act on. This is honestly most of the job.

## Speaking

I speak at conferences and events on data strategy, knowledge graphs, RAG, AI in the public sector, and the messy reality of making data work in the real world. Past events include EARL, LegalGeek, Humentum DC, MERL Tech, Charity Digital, LondonR, and AI Engineers UK.
