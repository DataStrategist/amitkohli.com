## Hello! Welcome!

My name is Amit, and I build data systems that make complex information usable.

My career has taken me from **UN water resources** mapping across 30+ countries, through **international development** organisations like ACDI/VOCA and CRS, into the world of **social care** at Access Social Care — where I now lead the data function.

Along the way I've been a data scientist, a trainer, a consultant, and (increasingly) the person raising the human concerns about technology. I describe myself as a **data generalist** and **systems thinker** — I build knowledge graphs, design ontologies, wrangle messy data, and think hard about how AI actually helps the people it's supposed to serve.

I love [R](https://www.r-project.org/), I'm paying close attention to knowledge graphs and LLMs, and when I'm not working I'm either building things with CNC mills, playing video games, or trying to find someone to throw a frisbee at.
